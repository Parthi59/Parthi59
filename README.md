### `02` [SYRAA Trust Engine V2](https://syraa-ai-trust-engine-v2.vercel.app)

**Document answers with evidence checks**

`Python` · `FastAPI` · `Sentence Transformers` · `Groq API` · `RAG`

I independently built SYRAA to help users inspect whether an AI-generated answer is supported by an uploaded document. It brings PDF processing, retrieval and answer verification into one application.

- Extracts and chunks document text, creates embeddings and retrieves relevant passages.
- Generates answers with citation-quality checks and claim-level verification.
- Presents trust scores, hallucination-risk indicators and downloadable reports.

The scores support human review; they do not guarantee that an answer is correct.

[Open application](https://syraa-ai-trust-engine-v2.vercel.app) · [View code](https://github.com/Parthi59/syraa-ai-trust-engine-V2)

<br/>

### `03` [IdeaZen AI](https://ideazen.vercel.app)

**From project ideas to implementation blueprints**

`React` · `TypeScript` · `Tailwind CSS` · `Groq API`

A team-built platform that recommends projects based on a user’s skills, interests and available time. Its two-stage workflow generates options first, then develops the selected idea into a structured blueprint with features, technology recommendations and a roadmap.

**My contribution:** AI integration and backend flow during my Elite Forums internship.

The platform also includes **Hackathon Mode**. I co-authored the published paper describing its two-stage generation architecture.

[Open application](https://ideazen.vercel.app) · [Read the paper](https://rjwave.org/ijedr/papers/IJEDR2601408.pdf)
